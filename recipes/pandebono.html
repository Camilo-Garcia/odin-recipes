Pandebono: Traditional Colombian Cheese Bread

Pandebonos are soft, cheesy Colombian breads with a lightly crisp exterior and a deliciously chewy center. A favorite in Cali, they are traditionally enjoyed warm at breakfast or as an afternoon snack with coffee, hot chocolate or your favorite drink. It's made of different doughs and cheese and fresh out of the oven the smell and taste makes your mouth-watering.

Ingredients:

To prepare this delicious dish we will need the following ingredients:

  • 2 cups cassava starch (also called tapioca starch or yuca starch).
  • 1 cup finely grated Colombian costeño cheese or another salty, firm fresh cheese.
  • 1/2 cup finely grated mozzarella cheese..
  • 1/4 cup pre-cooked cornmeal (masarepa).
  • 1 egg.
  • 1 - 3 tablespoon milk, as needed.
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)..

Step-by-Step Preparation:

Now we are going to see how to make Pandebono.

  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat your oven to 200 °C (400 °F). Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients: In a large bowl, combine the cassava starch, cornmeal, grated cheeses, and optional sugar.
  3. Add the egg: Add the egg and mix thoroughly until the dough begins to come together.
  4. Knead the dough: Gradually add milk, one tablespoon at a time, until you obtain a soft, smooth dough that holds its shape without crumbling or becoming sticky.
  5. Shape the pandebonos: Divide the dough into 10-12 portions and roll them into small balls or make ring shape. Place them on the prepared tray, leaving space between each other.
  6. Bake until golden: Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the pandebonos are puffed and lightly golden on the outside.
  7. Serve warm: Allow them to cool slightly before serving. Enjoy fresh from the oven with coffee, hot chocolate, or your favorite drink.
