Pandebono

Pandebono is a Colombian bread cheese, that it's perfect for eat with your coffee at mornings or in the afternoons. It's made of different doughs and cheese and fresh out of the oven the smell and taste makes your mouth-watering.

To prepare this delicious dish we will need the following ingredients:

  • 125 grams of cornstarch (1cup).
  • 125 grams of cassava starch (yuca or bitter manioc starch).
  • 250 grams of firm, flavorful cheese.
  • 1 tablespoon of baking powder.
  • 2 tablespoons of sugar.
  • 2 tablespoons of butter or margarine.
  • 1 egg.
  • 60 milliliters of milk (1/4 cup).
