Colmbian Empanadas, Crispy Corn Pockets:

Colombian Empanadas famous for their crunchy corn crust and savory filling. In Cali and the Valle del Cauca region, they are commonly filled with seasoned meat and potatoes and served with fresh ají sauce and lime.

Ingredients:

To prepare this delicious dish we will need the following ingredients:

For the filling:

  • 250 g beef, finely shredded or minced.
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced.
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil.
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped.
  • 2 green onions, finely chopped.
  • 1 garlic clove, minced.
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin.
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste.

For the Dough:

  • 2 cups pre-cooked yellow cornmeal (masarepa).
  • Approximately 2-1/2 cups warm water, or as needed.
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil.
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt.
  • A pinch of ground achiote or turmeric (optional for color).
  • Vegetable oil, for deep-frying.

Step-by-Step Preparation:

Now we are going to see how to prepare empanadas.

  1. Cook the potatoes: Boil the diced potatoes in salted water until tender. Drain and set aside.
  2. Prepare the meat: Cook the beef in water until tender, then shred it if using a whole cut. If using minced beef, cook it thoroughly in a pan.
  3. Make the filling:Heat the oil in a skillet. Add the green onions, tomatoes, and garlic. Cook until softened. Add the beef, potatoes, cumin, salt and papper. Mix well, gently mash some of the potatoes, and allow the filling to cool.
  4. Prepare the dough: Mix the cornmeal, salt, oil, and optional achiote. Gradually add warm water, kneading until the dough is soft, smooth, and easy to shape. Let it rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Shape the empanadas:Place a ball of dough between two sheets of plastic. Flatten it into a thin circle. Add a spoonful of filling to one half, fold the dough over and seal the edges firmly into a half-moon shape.
  6. Fry until crispy:Heat the oil to approximately 175 °C (350 °F). Fry the empanadas in batches until golden and crisp, about 4-6 minutes depending on their size. Drain on paper towels and ensure the filling is piping hot.
  7. Serve with ají:Enjoy hot with Colombian ají sauce and fresh lime wedges.
