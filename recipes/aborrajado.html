Aborrajados: Sweet Plantains Stuffed with Cheese.

Aborrajados are a beloved specialty from the VAlle del Cauca region. Made with sweet, ripe plantains filled with melted cheese and coated in a light batter, they are crispy on the outside and soft and sweet on the inside.

Ingredients:

To prepare this delicious dish we will need the following ingredients:

  • 2 very ripe plantains (yellow with dark spots).
  • 200 g mozzarella or Colombian fresh cheese, cut into strips.
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1/4 cup milk, approximately.
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (optional).
  • A pinch of salt.
  • Vegetable oil, for frying.

Step-by-Step Preparation:

Now we are going to see how to prepare aborrajados.

  1. Prepare the plantains: Peel the plantains and cut each one into two or three thick pieces.
  2. Cook the plantains: Frey the pieces in medium-hoot oil until golden and tender. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  3. Flatten and fill: While still warm, gently flatten each piece. Place a strip of cheese in the center and fold the plantain around it, sealing the edges as much as possible.
  4. MAke the batter: In a bowl, whisk together the flour, egg, milk, sugar (if using), and salt until you have a smooth, thick batter.
  5. Coat the aborrajados: Dip each stuffed plantain into the batter making sure it is evenly coated.
  6. Fry until golden: Fry in hot oil until the coating is crisp and golden and the cheese is melted. Drain on paper towels.
  7. Serve and enjoy: Serve warm as a snack, appetizer, or traditional Colombian treat.
